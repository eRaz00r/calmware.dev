Policies & Warranty

Micro-Task Policy

Quick, simple jobs that do not require a full feature.

  • Price: £30 each, or available as packs.
  • Scope: One small change, tiny adjustment, or minor fix that takes a short focused time.
  • Process: If a request is too large for a micro-task, Calmware will say so before starting and suggest the right Feature Ticket instead.
NOT a micro-task: New features, larger adjustments involving several parts of the app, changes that take more than a short focused session, or anything needing a new section/screens.

14-Day Feature Warranty

For any feature I deliver, I include a 14-day bug warranty.

Covers:
  • Fixes for issues caused directly by the new work delivered in a Feature Ticket.
  • Adjustments if the delivered feature does not match the agreed description.

Does NOT Cover:
  • Problems that existed before Calmware started work.
  • New ideas or changes in direction after delivery.
  • Issues in other, unrelated parts of the app.
  • Problems caused by outside services or systems.

After 14 days, fixes are handled as micro-tasks or as a new Feature Ticket.
