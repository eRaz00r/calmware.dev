Pricing & Services

Simple. Predictable. Quietly effective.

1. Feature Tickets (Fixed-Price Work)

A Feature Ticket is a clearly defined piece of work delivered at a fixed price.
You tell me what you want. I deliver it — calmly, clearly, and without surprises.

Ticket Size What it usually covers Price
Small Feature Ticket A small improvement or simple addition £100–£150
Medium Feature Ticket A new section or more detailed feature £200–£350
Large Feature Ticket A full feature with several steps or screens £400–£750

Every Feature Ticket includes:

  • A clear description of what will be delivered
  • A fixed price agreed upfront
  • A delivery timeframe
  • A short walkthrough video
  • A 14-day warranty for issues directly related to the new feature

2. Micro-Tasks (Small Fixes)

For quick, simple fixes that don’t require a full feature.

Pricing:
  • £30 per micro-task
  • £75 for 3
  • £120 for 5

A micro-task is:

  • One small change
  • A tiny adjustment in behaviour or layout
  • Something that takes very little time

Not a micro-task:

  • New features
  • Larger adjustments
  • Anything bigger than a short, focused fix

If something needs more than a micro-task, I’ll let you know before starting.

3. Calmware Support Plan

For clients who want steady, ongoing support.

£150–£250 per month

Includes:
  • Priority responses
  • Up to 4 micro-tasks per month
  • Regular checks to keep the app running smoothly
  • Help reviewing issues
  • Small improvements over time

The goal is simple: peace of mind.

4. Warranty & Issue Policy

14-Day Feature Warranty

Every delivered feature includes:

  • ✔ Fixes for anything caused by the new work
  • ✔ Adjustments if the feature does not match the agreed description

Not covered:

  • ✘ Problems that existed before the work
  • ✘ New ideas added after delivery
  • ✘ Issues in unrelated parts of the app
  • ✘ Problems caused by outside systems

After 14 days, fixes are handled as micro-tasks or through a new Feature Ticket.
